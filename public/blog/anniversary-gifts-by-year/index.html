Anniversary gifts by year: the traditional list, used well

Updated November 24, 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer

Anniversary gifts by year follow a traditional list of materials: paper for the first, cotton for the second, leather for the third, wood for the fifth, tin or aluminum for the tenth, silver for the twenty-fifth and gold for the fiftieth. Use the material as a creative prompt rather than a rule. It narrows an impossible choice down to something you can decide in minutes.

What are the anniversary gifts by year?

The traditional list gives each wedding anniversary a material, starting with paper for the first year and ending with diamond for the sixtieth. A modern list, added in the twentieth century, swaps in newer themes like clocks and appliances. Both versions appear, with small variations, in references such as The Knot's milestone list and MasterClass's year by year guide. Here is the version most US lists agree on, with a column for what each material can reasonably turn into.

Why a random material actually helps

Being told your fifth anniversary is about wood sounds arbitrary. It is, and that is why it works. "Buy something for our anniversary" is an impossible brief, because every object ever made qualifies. "Buy something wooden" is a brief you can act on in ten minutes. It removes almost every option and makes you inventive with what is left.

So treat the material as a theme, not a rule. Year six is iron: that can be a skillet, a day at a blacksmithing class, an iron trellis for the garden, or a joke about how the marriage is holding up. Any of those beats a generic gift card, because the constraint made you think about the two of you specifically.

Our picks for the most popular years

Some years have obvious, well-made gifts that fit the theme and get used for decades. These are the ones we would start with.

  1. Year 1, paper. A Fujifilm Instax Mini 12 turns the year ahead into a stack of paper prints, and it is simple enough that neither of you needs instructions. Add a handwritten letter and you have covered paper twice.
  2. Year 2, cotton. Good sheets are the cotton gift couples notice every night. A Brooklinen Luxe Core Sheet Set in sateen is soft and well reviewed, and it is the kind of upgrade most people put off buying for themselves.
  3. Year 3, leather. A slim wallet like the Bellroy Hide and Seek replaces the bulging one in his back pocket, and a leather cover for a Leuchtturm1917 notebook suits a partner who writes things down.
  4. Year 5, wood. A John Boos maple cutting board is thick, handsome and lasts for decades with a little oil. It sits on the counter as a daily reminder.
  5. Year 6, iron. A Lodge 6 quart enameled cast iron Dutch oven handles stews, bread and braises, and it is a gift to the cook in the relationship that will outlast the kitchen it lives in.
  6. Year 7, wool. A Pendleton wool blanket is warm, beautiful and made to be handed down, which is a decent metaphor for year seven.
  7. Year 10, aluminum. The Nordic Ware Naturals half sheet pan is plain aluminum, used in countless home kitchens, and makes a funny, useful anchor for a bigger tenth anniversary gift.
  8. Year 15, crystal. A pair of Riedel wine glasses gets used every time you open a bottle together.

Budget bands vary a lot here. The camera, wallet, sheet pan and glasses usually land under $100. The board, Dutch oven, sheets and blanket often run $100 to $250 depending on size.

How do you stretch an awkward material?

The years people dread, like tin, bronze and pottery, are often the easiest, because nobody expects a literal reading and the leap is where the charm lives.

Paper is the most flexible year on the list: letters, tickets, books, art, maps, photos, or a handwritten version of something you always say to each other. Cotton covers anything textile. Tin works for anything that comes in one, like good olive oil, tinned fish from a serious producer, or a vintage advertising tin. Bronze can be a small sculpture, bookends, or a trip to see a statue you both love. Pottery is perfect for an experience: book a wheel throwing class and make your own mugs, then use them for the next ten years.

Wood has one lovely option the table does not quite capture: plant a tree somewhere you will go back to. It is the rare anniversary gift that keeps growing.

When should you ignore the list?

Drop it when you already know the thing they want, when the material would push you into a gift neither of you cares about, or when what the relationship needs this year is time rather than an object.

That last case is more common than the list admits. Plenty of anniversaries land in years where both people are exhausted, and the right gift is two days away with phones off, or a weekend where someone else handles everything. No material covers that, and no material should stop you doing it.

A few gifts work at any year: printed photos (most couples have thousands of images and no physical copies), a return to somewhere significant like the restaurant from the first date, letters to each other sealed for a future anniversary, and an upgrade to something you both use every day. When you are both buying

Anniversaries differ from birthdays in one way: you are both marking the same occasion at the same moment, so a mismatch is very visible. One person produces a weekend away and the other produces a card, and the difference sits in the room.

The fix is unglamorous and reliable. Agree on the scale in advance, not the item. "Are we doing something big this year or keeping it small?" takes ten seconds and removes the most common source of anniversary disappointment, which is not a bad gift but an unequal one. Also decide whether you are buying for each other or buying something together. Plenty of couples find a joint gift, like a trip or a piece of furniture you both wanted, suits a long relationship better than two separate objects.

First anniversaries, milestones and hard years

First anniversaries of any kind. The list assumes a wedding, but it works just as well for the anniversary of getting together. For a first year, the strongest gifts document the year rather than making a statement about the future: a photo book of the twelve months, a map with the places you went marked, framed ticket stubs, a letter about what changed. Our guide to a first Christmas in a new relationship covers the same balance between thoughtful and too much.

Milestones. The tenth, twenty-fifth and fiftieth carry expectations, and the trap is thinking the answer is simply to spend more. What makes a milestone gift is that it marks elapsed time: something involving the people who were there at the start, a commissioned piece from an artist, the story written down, or the trip you have talked about for years.

Hard years. Anniversaries arrive whether or not life has cooperated. A short, honest acknowledgment, like a letter or a meal neither of you had to cook, is a complete answer to a difficult year. There is no obligation to put on a celebration on schedule.

Frequently asked questions

What are the traditional anniversary gifts by year?

The most common US sequence runs 1 paper, 2 cotton, 3 leather, 4 fruit or flowers, 5 wood, 6 iron or candy, 7 wool or copper, 8 bronze, 9 pottery, 10 tin or aluminum, then 15 crystal, 20 china, 25 silver, 30 pearl, 40 ruby, 50 gold and 60 diamond.

What is the difference between traditional and modern anniversary gifts?

Modern lists swap in newer materials, such as clocks for year one, china for year two and appliances for year four. They were added largely as a commercial update. Neither list is official, so use whichever one gives you the better idea.

What is a good first anniversary gift?

Paper is the traditional first anniversary material, and it is more flexible than it sounds: a handwritten letter, concert tickets, a printed photo book of the year, a framed map of where you met, or an instant camera like the Fujifilm Instax Mini 12 for paper prints.

Do you have to follow the anniversary gift list?

No. The list has no official status, and many couples ignore it. Its real value is as a prompt, because something made of wood is a far easier brief than something for our fifth anniversary. Skip it when you already know what they want.

What is the anniversary gift for 10 years?

The traditional tenth anniversary material is tin or aluminum and the modern one is diamond. Most couples treat it as a milestone and pair a playful tin or aluminum gift with a trip, a piece of jewelry or something for the home.
